About Charleston Foodie

Charleston Foodie is a guide to the first half of the day on the Charleston peninsula. Dinner here gets plenty of coverage. Breakfast gets less, and it is the meal most visitors and locals actually eat out the most, so that is the one we write about.

What We Cover

Breakfast comes first, with a particular interest in kitchens that serve it all day. Brunch and lunch come along because downtown's daytime restaurants rarely draw a line between them. The all-day breakfast guide is the main list, sorted by neighborhood, and the breakfast and lunch page explains how to order across a full morning.

How the List Is Built

Every listing is on the peninsula, reachable on foot or by a short ride from King Street, and gets an address and a specific order rather than a general recommendation. We keep the list short on purpose. A place stays on it when the food is consistent, the service is quick enough for a weekday, and the menu works at more than one hour of the day.

Keeping It Current

Restaurants change hours, menus, and sometimes addresses. Each listing links to the restaurant's own website, which is the right place to confirm details before you go. The date at the top of each guide shows when it was last updated.
